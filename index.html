The problem

A feedback board shouldn’t cost hundreds a month

You want users to vote, you ship, you post a changelog. That should not scale into a usage-based bill every time engagement grows.

Canny’s tracked-user model (anyone who posts, votes, or comments counts) looks cheap at the door, then scales hard.

FAQ

Quick answers

Is this a Canny clone?

Same job-to-be-done: public feedback, voting, roadmap, changelog. Different promise: flat price and unlimited users. We’re not matching every enterprise feature on day one.

What does “unlimited users” mean?

Voters and commenters don’t count against a meter. You pay for the plan, not for how many customers engage.

Will price go up later?

Founding members who pay via the Stripe link keep the founding rate for year 1 (or locked first year — we’ll state which before charge). After that, standard Pro/Team. Waitlist-only folks get launch pricing when we go live.

Can I import from Canny?

CSV import is on the waitlist form as a demand check. If enough founders want it, it ships early. No promise of a full live sync on day one.

Who is this for?

Indie founders, small SaaS teams, and solo PMs who need a feedback board without a usage-based bill. If you need Autopilot-from-Gong, SSO, and custom admin roles, Canny/others may still fit better.
